Idempotency Protection

Payment creation requires an idempotency key, with conflicting reuse rejected and concurrent duplicate creation protected.

◆

Credential Lifecycle

API secrets are hashed, hidden after issuance, rotatable and revocable. Environment mismatches are rejected.

✓

Webhook Integrity

Provider events and outbound webhook deliveries use integrity controls, duplicate protection and retry safeguards.

↔

Payment State Safety

Illegal status downgrades and invalid payment lifecycle transitions are rejected by the backend.

□

Provider Validation

Providers can be activated or deactivated and validated through protected administrative endpoints.

!

Account Suspension

Suspended businesses and inactive users are blocked from protected payment operations by backend authorization checks.
