Payment Links

Give customers a simple way to pay without requiring a custom checkout integration. PrimePay payment links are planned as a shareable payment experience for websites, social channels, messages and direct customer requests.

Planned payment-link flow

The experience below describes the intended product flow, not an active V1 backend capability.

01
Create Configure the amount, currency and customer-facing payment details.
02
Share Send the generated payment destination to your customer.
03
Pay The customer completes payment through the supported payment flow.
04
Track Monitor the resulting payment and transaction status in PrimePay.
V1 capability notice Payment-link creation, retrieval, activation, expiration and public checkout endpoints are not currently exposed by the PrimePay V1 backend. This page therefore does not create or display fictional links. The currently supported payment creation workflow is available through the Payments page.
